为什么选择词境Why LexiVista?

📥 导入自己的单词与文章Bring your own words

粘贴、选择文件或拍照识别，自动查音标、释义和例句，并翻译成中文。文章逐句中英对照。

Paste, pick a file or scan a photo. Get phonetics, definitions, examples and Chinese translations; articles are translated sentence by sentence.

✨ 场景联想Scene vocabulary

描述一个生活场景——机场值机、看医生、家长会——自动联想你会用到的单词和常用句。

Describe a real-life scene — airport check-in, a doctor's visit, a parent-teacher meeting — and get the words and phrases you'll actually need.

💬 AI 情景对话AI role-play

和 AI 在场景中对话练习，自动穿插场景词汇，并用中文给出语法纠正。

Practise a conversation in the scene with AI, using your scene words, with gentle corrections in Chinese.

🎧 朗读Read-aloud

单词优先真人发音，支持美式、英式、澳式口音；单词本和整篇文章可以连续朗读，并可同时读出中文。

Human pronunciation for words, US/UK/AU accents, and continuous playback of word lists and whole articles — with optional Chinese.

🏞️ 动态风景背景Scenic backgrounds

12 张精选风景，可固定、每日轮换或随机，也可以用每日在线风景或自己的照片。

12 hand-made scenic backgrounds — fixed, daily or random — plus a daily online landscape or your own photo.

🔁 间隔复习Spaced review

闪卡、选择题和听力测验，按记忆曲线安排下一次复习。

Flashcards, multiple-choice and listening quizzes, scheduled on a spaced-repetition curve.
