常见问题Frequently Asked Questions

需要注册账号吗？Do I need an account?

不需要。打开就能用，所有单词本和学习记录都保存在你的设备上。

No. LexiVista works without an account; your word lists and progress are stored on your device.

怎么导入自己的单词或词组？How do I import my own words?

打开「词库」→「导入单词/词组」，可以粘贴、选择 .txt/.csv 文件或拍照识别。每行一个，或用逗号分隔；在单词后加上自己的中文（如 itinerary: 行程）会优先使用你的释义。

Go to Library › Import Words. Paste text, pick a .txt/.csv file, or scan a photo. One term per line or comma-separated; add your own Chinese after the word (e.g. itinerary: 行程) to use your meaning.

怎么导入文章并朗读？How do I import and listen to an article?

「词库」→「导入文章」，粘贴或拍照后会逐句翻译。在文章页点右上角播放按钮整篇朗读；轻点句子单独朗读，长按句子可以查词并加入单词本。

Library › Import Article. It's translated sentence by sentence. Tap play to read the whole article; tap a sentence to hear it, long-press to look up words and save them.

场景联想是怎么工作的？How does scene vocabulary work?

在「场景」页描述一个场景（中英文都可以），App 会联想相关的单词和常用句，你可以勾选后保存，再用闪卡、测验或 AI 对话练习。

On the Scenes tab, describe a situation in Chinese or English. LexiVista suggests related words and phrases; save the ones you want and practise with flashcards, quizzes or AI chat.

怎么换背景？How do I change the background?

每个页面右上角的图片按钮可以一键换背景；在「我的 › 页面背景」里可以选择固定、每日轮换、随机，以及调节暗化、模糊和运动效果。每日在线风景和「我的照片」为 Pro 功能。

Tap the picture button at the top right of any page to switch instantly, or go to Me › Background for fixed / daily / random modes, dimming, blur and motion. Daily online landscapes and your own photo are Pro features.

朗读声音不够自然怎么办？The voice sounds robotic — what can I do?

在 iPhone「设置 › 辅助功能 › 朗读内容 › 声音」中下载“增强”或“优质”英文语音，词境会自动使用。也可以在「我的 › 朗读设置」调整口音和语速。

Download an Enhanced or Premium English voice in iOS Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices; LexiVista uses it automatically. Adjust accent and speed in Me › Speech.

免费版和 Pro 有什么区别？What's the difference between Free and Pro?

免费版包含 5 个单词本（每本 50 个词条）、3 篇文章、3 个场景、每天 5 轮 AI 对话和 4 张风景背景。Pro 去除所有数量限制，并解锁全部风景、每日在线风景和自定义照片背景。

Free includes 5 word lists (50 entries each), 3 articles, 3 scenes, 5 AI chat turns per day and 4 backgrounds. Pro removes all limits and unlocks every background, the daily online landscape and your own photo.

怎么取消订阅？How do I cancel my subscription?

订阅由 Apple ID 管理：iPhone「设置 › 你的名字 › 订阅 › LexiVista › 取消订阅」。Pro 权益会保留到当前计费周期结束。

Subscriptions are managed by your Apple ID: iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions › LexiVista › Cancel. Pro stays active until the end of the current period.

换手机后怎么恢复购买？How do I restore my purchase?

使用购买时的 Apple ID 登录 App Store，然后在「我的 › 升级 Pro」页面底部点「恢复购买」。

Sign in with the Apple ID you purchased with, then open Me › Upgrade to Pro and tap Restore Purchases.

怎么删除我的数据？How do I delete my data?

长按单词本、场景或文章即可删除。卸载 App 会删除所有本机数据。我们没有服务器，不保存你的学习数据。

Long-press a word list, scene or article to delete it. Uninstalling removes all local data. We run no servers and keep none of your learning data.

我的数据安全吗？Is my data private?

你的单词本、文章和进度只保存在本机。翻译、查词和 AI 功能会把你提交的文字发送给相应服务，详见 隐私政策。

Your lists, articles and progress stay on your device. Translation, lookup and AI features send the text you submit to those services — see the Privacy Policy.

词境和 OpenWord 是什么关系？How is LexiVista related to OpenWord?

词境是 OpenWord 旗下的场景学习应用，由同一团队开发。OpenWord 侧重 5,000 个核心高频词和 3,000+ 日常短语，词境侧重你自己的内容和生活场景。两个 App 需分别下载和订阅。

LexiVista is part of the OpenWord family, made by the same team. OpenWord focuses on 5,000 core words and 3,000+ everyday phrases; LexiVista focuses on your own content and real-life scenes. The apps are downloaded and subscribed to separately.
