隐私政策Privacy Policy · LexiVista 词境

最后更新 Last updated: October 25, 2026

简要说明：LexiVista（词境）把你的单词本、文章和学习进度只保存在你的设备上。无需注册账号，没有广告，我们不出售你的数据。为了翻译、查词和 AI 场景联想，你主动提交的文字会发送给下文列出的第三方服务。

The short version: LexiVista keeps your word lists, articles and learning progress on your device. No account is required, there are no ads, and we never sell your data. To translate, look up words and generate scene vocabulary, the text you submit is sent to the third-party services listed below.

1. 我们收集的信息Information We Collect

  • 学习数据：你导入或生成的单词、词组、文章、场景、复习记录和掌握度，使用 Apple SwiftData 保存在本机。 Learning data: words, phrases, articles, scenes, review history and mastery you import or generate are stored on your device with Apple's SwiftData.
  • 偏好设置：朗读口音与语速、背景选择等设置保存在本机。 Preferences: speech accent and rate, background choices and similar settings are stored on your device.
  • 购买信息：订阅状态由 Apple App Store（StoreKit）提供，我们看不到你的支付信息。 Purchase information: subscription status comes from Apple's App Store via StoreKit. We never see your payment details.
  • 不收集：我们不要求账号，不收集姓名、邮箱、位置或通讯录，也不使用任何分析或广告追踪 SDK。 Not collected: no account, name, email, location or contacts, and no analytics or advertising trackers.

2. 信息的使用方式How We Use Your Information

本机数据仅用于展示你的单词本、文章和学习进度，安排复习，以及根据你的偏好朗读和显示背景。

On-device data is used only to show your word lists, articles and progress, schedule reviews, and apply your speech and background preferences.

3. 发送给第三方服务的内容Content Sent to Third-Party Services

以下功能需要联网。只有你主动输入或导入的文字会被发送，不包含账号或设备标识。

These features need the internet. Only the text you enter or import is sent; no account or device identifiers are included.

These services are governed by their own privacy policies. Please don't enter sensitive personal information into translation or chat.

4. 数据共享Data Sharing

除第 3 条所述的功能请求外，我们不会出售、出租或交易你的信息。订阅由 Apple 处理，受 Apple 隐私政策 约束。

Apart from the feature requests in Section 3, we do not sell, rent or trade your information. Subscriptions are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. 照片与文字识别Photos & Text Recognition

只有你主动选择的照片会被读取：用作背景的照片保存在本机；用于「拍照识别」的照片在设备上用 Apple Vision 识别文字，不会上传。

Only photos you pick are read. A background photo is stored on your device; photos used for "scan text" are processed on-device with Apple Vision and are not uploaded.

6. 朗读Audio & Text-to-Speech

朗读使用 Apple 设备端语音合成（AVSpeechSynthesizer），在本机生成。单词的真人发音音频从 Free Dictionary API 下载并缓存在本机。

Read-aloud uses Apple's on-device AVSpeechSynthesizer. Human pronunciation clips are downloaded from the Free Dictionary API and cached on your device.

7. 数据存储与安全Data Storage & Security

你的学习数据保存在 iOS 设备的应用沙盒中，不会上传到我们的服务器（我们没有服务器）。数据安全很大程度上取决于设备本身的安全设置。

Your learning data stays in the app's sandbox on your iOS device and is not uploaded to us (we run no servers). Its security largely depends on the security of your device.

8. 儿童隐私Children's Privacy

LexiVista 不会有意收集 13 岁以下儿童的个人信息，也不包含针对儿童收集身份信息的功能。

LexiVista does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 and has no features that collect identifying information from children.

9. 你的权利Your Rights

  • 查看：所有数据都可以在 App 内查看。Access: all your data is visible in the app.
  • 修改：单词释义、例句可在单词详情页编辑。Edit: meanings and examples can be edited on each word's page.
  • 删除：长按单词本、场景或文章即可删除；卸载 App 会删除所有本机数据。Deletion: long-press a word list, scene or article to delete it; uninstalling the app removes all local data.

10. 政策变更Changes to This Policy

我们可能会不时更新本政策，并修改页面顶部的“最后更新”日期。重大变更会在 App 内告知。

We may update this policy from time to time and will revise the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will be communicated in the app.

Questions about this policy? Contact us at thevsx.tech@gmail.com.
